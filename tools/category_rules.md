# Amar Natore — category, caption and poster rules (shared by the page and profile tasks)

## Research
Use WebSearch (standard mode) and confirm every fact from at least one reputable page you actually open with WebFetch. Never invent facts, numbers, prices, dates, quotes, hadith, prayer times or links.

## Categories (rotation order)
natore → ai → health → jobs → sports → market → english → islam → govt_service → phone → scholarship → scam_alert → recipe → career → exam → computer → freelancing → branding → heritage → finance → agri → success_story → day_history → parenting → (back to natore)

- natore: latest news about Natore district (Natore Sadar, Singra, Baraigram, Gurudaspur, Lalpur, Bagatipara, Naldanga) from established Bangladeshi outlets (Prothom Alo, Daily Star, bdnews24, Kaler Kantho, Jugantor, Samakal, Dhaka Tribune, Natore local outlets). Prefer development, education, agriculture, weather, events, public services, achievements. Avoid party politics, crime/accident victims' names or photos, rumours, communal topics. Story from the last ~3 days.
- ai: latest AI news, a useful AI tool/tip, or what top AI accounts on X are saying (via news coverage; credit "সূত্র: @handle (X)" only if the source clearly says so; translate at most one short line).
- health: general wellness/prevention/seasonal health info from WHO, DGHS Bangladesh, icddr,b, CDC, NHS or major hospitals. No medicine names with doses, no diagnosis, no miracle cures. End with "সমস্যা হলে রেজিস্টার্ড ডাক্তারের পরামর্শ নিন।"
- jobs: current Bangladesh job circulars, prioritising GOVERNMENT jobs whose deadline has NOT passed. Verify on the official site or teletalk.com.bd / alljobs.teletalk.com.bd. Include organisation, post, number of posts, qualification summary, deadline, official link.
- sports: latest cricket and football results/fixtures from ESPNcricinfo, Cricbuzz, BCB, BBC Sport, ESPN, official league sites. Scores must match the source. Score layout for results.
- market: current prices of daily essentials from TCB's daily price list, DAM or major newspapers' market reports (last 3 days); state the date and that prices are Dhaka/market averages unless a Natore/Rajshahi price is reported. Ranges exactly as the source.
- english: practical corporate/spoken English — email/meeting phrases, common mistakes, interview answers. Original; English line + Bangla meaning.
- islam: one authentic hadith (collection + number) explained in the light of 1-2 related Quran ayahs (সূরা, আয়াত), verified on sunnah.com / hadeethenc.com and quran.com. Only sahih/hasan. Respectful, non-sectarian; no fatwa, controversial fiqh or politics. Quote layout with ayah + ayah_ref.
- govt_service: step-by-step guide to a Bangladeshi public service verified on the official portal (nidw.gov.bd, epassport.gov.bd, bdris.gov.bd, land.gov.bd, mutation.land.gov.bd, etaxnbr.gov.bd, bangladesh.gov.bd) — fees/documents exactly as stated; official link.
- phone: new/trending phone in Bangladesh — official BD price and key specs; "সম্ভাব্য দাম" if unofficial; if sources disagree on the official price, pick another phone.
- scholarship: a currently open scholarship Bangladeshi students can apply for. Search widely (Scholarship Positions, Opportunity Desk, Opportunities for Youth, Scholars4Dev, ScholarshipPortal, Mastersportal, FindAPhD, official programme sites — Chevening, Commonwealth, GREAT, Fulbright, DAAD, Erasmus Mundus, Stipendium Hungaricum, MEXT, JDS, GKS, Türkiye Bursları, CSC, Australia Awards, Swedish Institute, Campus France, universities — and Bangladeshi sources). Prefer fully funded, deadline ≥ 7 days away, Bangladesh explicitly eligible, no programme repeated within 30 days. Confirm on the OFFICIAL page; "expected" cycles are not posted. Include eligibility, benefits, deadline, official link.
- scam_alert: current fraud/cyber-safety warning for Bangladesh from a recent news report or official notice (Bangladesh Bank, BTRC, CTTC, bKash/Nagad); protective steps; helpline only if verified (999 for emergencies). No victims' names.
- recipe: one dish from a different country each time (sometimes Bangladeshi regional) — ingredients and short steps in your own words + a tip.
- career: corporate job-life growth — productivity, communication, promotions, interviews, salary negotiation, work-life balance.
- exam: SSC/HSC routines, results, admission tests, university circulars, board notices — official boards/universities or major newspapers, last 3 days; dates exactly as stated.
- computer: practical skills — Excel formulas, Word/PowerPoint, Google Sheets, shortcuts, basic cybersecurity, free courses. Step-by-step.
- freelancing: legitimate platforms, skills, free courses (government programmes only if verified), profile tips, receiving payments legally in Bangladesh, spotting scams. Never promise income amounts.
- branding: LinkedIn, CV, public speaking, online presence, networking, content creation.
- heritage: Natore's history, heritage, tourism (Uttara Gonobhaban, Natore Rajbari, Rani Bhabani, Chalan Beel, kacha golla, local places, crafts, festivals) verified from Banglapedia, natore.gov.bd or reputable newspapers.
- finance: savings certificates (National Savings Directorate), DPS basics, mobile-banking safety/charges from official pages, budgeting. Facts only; add "এটি আর্থিক পরামর্শ নয়; সিদ্ধান্তের আগে ব্যাংক/বিশেষজ্ঞের সাথে কথা বলুন।"
- agri: seasonal farming advice for the Natore region based on current weather and DAE, BARI, BRRI or AIS advice. No pesticide brand names or doses.
- success_story: positive story of a person from Natore or northern Bangladesh reported by a reputable newspaper; own words, credit, only what the article says.
- day_history: a national/international day today or within 2 days, or "আজকের দিনে ইতিহাসে", verified from un.org, Banglapedia or reputable sources. Respectful, non-partisan.
- parenting: study habits, screen time, nutrition basics, sleep, adolescent wellbeing — UNICEF, WHO, AAP or reputable hospitals. No medicine doses.

## Caption — Bangla (Bengali script), friendly, 120-220 words
- Line 1: hook with the main keyword + 1 emoji.
- Body: value first, in your OWN words; ✅ bullets or numbered steps; key facts exactly as the source.
- "সূত্র: <outlet/organisation>". For jobs, scholarship, exam, govt_service also "আবেদন/বিস্তারিত: <official URL>" and a line to check the official notice and beware of agents who ask for money. Other categories: no URLs.
- A question to drive comments + ask to share/save.
- 8-14 hashtags, Bangla + English, always including #AmarNatore #আমার_নাটোর #Natore #নাটোর.
- Write the caption to a file, re-read it for typos and wrong numbers, and make sure it is COMPLETE.
